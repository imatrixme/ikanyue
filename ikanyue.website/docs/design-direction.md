# Website Design Direction: Ticket Archive

The official website implements the selected Candidate 02 direction from the
2026-07-17 image-generation round.

## Visual Contract

- The first viewport is a bright full-bleed music image with the Kanyue name and
  a literal offer; hero copy is never placed inside a card.
- A visible portion of the next course section remains in the first viewport.
- Activities use vertical date rails, fine rules, and restrained perforations.
- Courses and faculty use a disciplined editorial grid rather than ticket styling
  everywhere.
- Page sections are full-width bands. Cards are reserved for repeated course,
  teacher, story, and event items and use no more than 6px radius.
- Kanyue green marks navigation, section rules, direct actions, and one contact
  band. Warm white and deep ink remain dominant.
- Images show real instruments, practice, performance, and learners. The copy and
  content structure explicitly serve children and adults.
- Motion is brief and state-driven and must respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Responsive Contract

- Desktop uses a 1180px content grid and 72px header.
- Mobile preserves date rails and ticket hierarchy without horizontal overflow.
- The supported visual-check widths are 320, 375, 768, 1280, and 1440 pixels.
